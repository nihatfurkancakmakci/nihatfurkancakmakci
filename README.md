## 👋 Hi, I'm Furkan

I'm an AI engineer who builds assistants that **talk, listen and answer from real data**, in writing and by voice.

Most chatbots sound confident even when they're wrong. I focus on the opposite: AI that a business can actually rely on. My assistants look things up through controlled tools instead of guessing, so the answers come from real data and not from imagination.

## 💡 What I'm Good At

- 🤖 **AI agents:** assistants that decide on their own which tool to use and when
- 🎙️ **Voice AI:** natural conversations you can speak to, not just type to
- 📚 **Connecting AI to company knowledge:** so it answers with your data, not generic internet answers
- 🛡️ **Trustworthy by design:** security, testing and monitoring built in from day one
- 🚢 **Idea to production:** I take projects from first prototype to a live system

## 🏗️ Featured Project

**A written and voice AI assistant for managers.** A manager can simply ask *"how is this month going?"* and get an answer from live operational data, by typing or by speaking.

I built it end to end: the chat and voice experience, the connection to the data, the security so every customer only sees their own information, and the testing that proves it keeps giving correct answers. I also compared different AI providers and costs to choose the right setup, then moved it from prototype to a running system.
